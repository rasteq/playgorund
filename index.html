Built by students, for students

The story of why we built a CGPA calculator that actually gets the math right.

2026
origin story

Gradzz started as a late-night side project because doing National University’s CGPA math by hand was genuinely not it.

Calculating grades across four years, ten distinct grade bands, and two different syllabuses manually is tedious and error-prone. We built a CGPA calculator that actually gets it right, and actually looks like something made in 2026.

Every department across BSc, BBA, BSS, and BA Honours is covered, for both the 2024–2025 and 2013–2014 syllabuses, plus the Degree Pass course. Everything is grade-weighted and calculated live as you type — no sign-up, no spreadsheet, no cap.

Gradzz is an independent educational project and isn't affiliated with National University Bangladesh — just students trying to make results day a little less stressful for everyone else.

// privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Your grades stay yours — no accounts, no server tracking, completely local.

LOCAL
Zero Server Storage

Gradzz doesn’t ask for an account, an email, or a password — so there’s nothing to leak in the first place. The grades you enter are saved only in your own browser’s session storage, on your own device, for as long as that browser tab stays open.

Nothing you type into the calculator is sent to a server, sold, or shared with anyone, including us. Closing the tab, or hitting the "Reset" button on a marksheet, clears that saved data for good.

01
01 · Zero Data Retention

What We Don't Collect

No accounts, no email addresses, no phone numbers, no passwords. We don’t run ad trackers, don’t build user profiles, and don’t sell or share data with advertisers — there simply isn’t any data to sell.

If you email us or message us through the Contact page, we only use that information to reply to your inquiry — never for unsolicited marketing, and never passed on to third parties.

02
02 · Client-Side Storage

How Session Storage Works

Your grades are stored using your browser's built-in session storage, tied strictly to that specific tab. They are not synced to any cloud database, so they won't automatically follow you to another phone, laptop, browser, or even a newly opened tab.

Closing the tab, using private/incognito mode, or hitting the "Reset" button on a marksheet permanently erases that saved data — we have no server copy to restore it from. Make sure to download your result as a verified PDF transcript before closing your tab.

03
03 · Infrastructure & Logs

Cookies & Analytics

Gradzz does not use tracking cookies or intrusive third-party analytics scripts. Like most static websites, our hosting infrastructure may automatically register basic technical server logs (such as request timestamps and general traffic volume) for security, DDoS prevention, and uptime diagnostics.

These network logs never contain your grades, student information, or any personal identifiers.

04
04 · Outbound Destinations

Third-Party Links

Occasional links (such as social links or contact emails) point outside Gradzz. We are not responsible for the privacy policies or practices of external services you access through those destinations — please review their respective terms independently.

05
05 · Scope & Audience

Target Audience

Gradzz is built specifically for current and prospective National University Bangladesh students, generally of adult university age. It is not engineered or marketed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly gather any data from them.

06
06 · Revisions

Policy Updates

Gradzz is an independent, non-commercial student project. This privacy statement may be updated as the platform adds new features; check back here anytime for the latest revision. Have questions? Reach out directly on the Contact page.
